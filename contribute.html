Submit a guideline

Do you know of a legislative drafting manual, style guide or set of drafting rules that is missing here, or a newer edition of one we list? Tell us about it. National, supranational and international bodies are the current focus; sub-national guidelines are welcome too.

Submitting opens a pre-filled issue on GitHub (a free GitHub account is needed). Your email is not included in the public issue.
