# Research backlog

Guidelines identified but not yet catalogued. Phase 1 covers national and supranational bodies.

## National, to locate or verify

- Ireland: Office of the Parliamentary Counsel to the Government (no public drafting manual found on gov.ie, 2026-10-29).
- Belgium: Conseil d'État / Raad van State, *Principes de technique législative – Guide de rédaction des textes législatifs et réglementaires* (official URL not yet confirmed).
- Portugal: legística rules annexed to the Regimento do Conselho de Ministros (confirm the current resolution).
- Brazil: *Manual de Redação da Presidência da República*, 3rd ed. 2018 (official planalto.gov.br URL not confirmed); Decreto nº 12.002/2024 on drafting of normative acts.
- Norway: *Lovteknikk og lovforberedelse* (Justis- og beredskapsdepartementet).
- Sweden: *Propositionshandboken* and *Författningshandboken* (Regeringskansliet).
- Estonia: *Hea õigusloome ja normitehnika eeskiri* (Government regulation) and the Ministry of Justice drafting handbook.
- Slovenia: *Nomotehnične smernice*; Slovakia: *Legislatívne pravidlá vlády*; Hungary, Lithuania, Latvia, Croatia, Romania, Bulgaria, Greece.
- India: Legislative Department drafting manual; South Africa: OCSLA guidance; Nigeria, Ghana, Malaysia, Singapore (AGC), Philippines.
- Japan (法制執務), Republic of Korea (법령 입안·심사 기준), Mexico, Argentina, Chile, Colombia.
- Hong Kong (DoJ, *Drafting Legislation in Hong Kong*) — special administrative region.

## Supranational and international, to locate

- Council of the European Union: *Manual of precedents for acts established within the Council*.
- European Commission Legal Service drafting guidance; EUR-Lex *Interinstitutional style guide* PDF edition.
- Council of Europe model final clauses; African Union; ECOWAS; CARICOM; ASEAN; Mercosur; Andean Community.
- OECD and World Bank guidance on regulatory drafting.
