# Research backlog

Status of guideline coverage across national, devolved, supranational, and international bodies.

## Completed in v2 (45+ guidelines added)

All items from the v1 backlog have been researched, verified with official URLs, and added to `data/guidelines.yml`:

- **Supranational & International**: OSCE/ODIHR (*Guidelines on Democratic Lawmaking for Better Laws*, 2024), Council of Europe / Venice Commission (*Rule of Law Checklist*, 2016), OECD (*Recommendation on Regulatory Policy and Governance*, 2012), UNCITRAL (*Legislative Guide on Insolvency Law* & *Model Law on Electronic Commerce*), Council of the European Union (*Manual of Precedents*), European Commission (*Better Regulation Guidelines* & *How to Write Clearly*), ASEAN (*Handbook on Good Regulatory Practice*).
- **United States**: US House Office of the Legislative Counsel (*Guide to Legislative Drafting* & *Quick Guide*), Uniform Law Commission (*Drafting Rules and Style Manual*, 2025 ed.).
- **UK Devolved & Crown Dependencies**: Scotland PCO (*Drafting Matters!*, 2nd ed.), Wales OLC (*Writing Laws for Wales*), UK Cabinet Office (*Statutory Instrument Practice*, 5th ed.), Jersey LDO (*Legislative Drafting Office Manual*).
- **Europe**: Ireland (LRC 61-2000), Belgium (Conseil d'État *Principes de technique législative* FR & NL), Portugal (*Guia Prático de Redação Legislativa* & *Regras de Legística*), Norway (*Lovteknikk og lovforberedelse*), Sweden (*Gröna boken Ds 2014:1*), Estonia (*HÕNTE*), Slovenia (*Nomotehnične smernice*), Slovakia (*Legislatívne pravidlá vlády SR*), Croatia (*Jedinstvena metodološko-nomotehnička pravila*), Hungary (*61/2009 IRM rendelet* & *Jszr.*), Romania (*Legea nr. 24/2000*), Bulgaria (*Указ № 883*), Greece (*Law 4622/2019* & *Manual of Legislative Drafting Methodology*), Cyprus, Lithuania, Latvia, Albania, Serbia, Georgia.
- **Americas**: Brazil (*Decreto nº 12.002/2024* & *Manual de Redação da Presidência da República* 3ª ed.), Argentina, Mexico, Colombia, Peru (Ministry of Justice & Congress manuals), Uruguay, Dominican Republic.
- **Asia-Pacific**: Hong Kong DoJ (*Drafting Legislation in Hong Kong* & *Bilingual Drafting*), Singapore AGC (*PLUS Plain Laws Understandable by Singaporeans*), South Korea MOLEG (*법령 입안·심사 기준*), Japan CLB (*法令用語・法制執務*), India Legislative Department (*Manual of Parliamentary Procedures*), Qatar.
- **Africa**: South Africa (*CSLS Plain Language Guide*), Nigeria (*NILS Manual*), Tanzania (*OCS Chief Parliamentary Draftsman's Manual*), Rwanda (*RLRC Manual*), Liberia (*Manual for Legislative Drafting*).
