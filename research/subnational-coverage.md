# Sub-national coverage

Research log for `data/subnational.yml`. Checked 2026-11-01.

Discovery sources: the CALC list of drafting manuals (https://www.calc.ngo/drafting-manuals-and-other-resources), legislature and government sites, and the University of Zurich Zentrum für Rechtsetzungslehre collection of cantonal guidelines. Entries listed by CALC carry a `credits` entry for CALC. Where a CALC link was stale, the entry records the current edition on the issuer's site and the CALC-listed edition in `currency.note`.

CALC's "New Mexico" row links Montana's 2016 manual. Both the Montana and the New Mexico entries carry the CALC credit.

## Searched, nothing found

Absence means nothing was found in the searches run, not that no guidance exists.

- **United States:** California, Georgia, Michigan, New York, Vermont, Nebraska (no bill or rules drafting manual published); Kansas has only a regulations filing manual. Oklahoma (binding drafting rules in OAC 655:10; rules.ok.gov blocked) and Texas (format rules in 1 TAC ch. 91; viewer moved) not added. Puerto Rico, Guam, US Virgin Islands, American Samoa, Northern Mariana Islands. Mississippi, Rhode Island, South Carolina and Wyoming have agency-rules drafting or format rules only, which are included.
- **Canada:** Ontario and Québec (only private-bill procedure guides, not included), Québec (only a 1984 SOQUIJ book, not online), Alberta (standards internal), Manitoba, New Brunswick, Saskatchewan, PEI, Newfoundland and Labrador, Yukon, Nunavut. The ULCC Drafting Conventions are intergovernmental; no live official URL found (1991 text reprinted in CALC's *The Loophole*, August 1991).
- **Australia:** South Australia, Northern Territory. The NSW *Manual for Members of Parliament for the Drafting of Non-Government Legislation* (13th ed., 2023) is procedural and was left out.
- **Germany:** Hessen, Niedersachsen (guidance intranet-only), Hamburg, Bremen, Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, Saarland, Thüringen, Rheinland-Pfalz.
- **Austria:** Wien, Tirol, Salzburg, Kärnten, Burgenland (follows the federal Legistische Richtlinien 1990).
- **Switzerland:** Jura, Nidwalden.
- **Belgium:** Wallonia, Brussels-Capital, French Community, German-speaking Community (use the Conseil d'État guide).
- **Spain:** Asturias (1992 directrices cited, not online), Castilla y León, Navarra, Illes Balears, Murcia, Extremadura, Cantabria, La Rioja, Castilla-La Mancha.
- **Italy:** Toscana, Emilia-Romagna, Lombardia, Veneto, Lazio, Friuli Venezia Giulia (use the interregional manual); Piemonte (2025 deliberation adopting the 4th edition; host unreachable), Bolzano (2024 clear-writing guidelines; host blocked), Sicily, Valle d'Aosta.
- **United Kingdom:** Northern Ireland Office of the Legislative Counsel guidance not published (executiveoffice-ni.gov.uk sitemap and NI Assembly site checked).
- **Bosnia and Herzegovina:** Brčko District.
- **Portugal:** Madeira.
- **Finland:** Åland.
- **Brazil:** most other states. Most states also have a binding complementary law on drafting (e.g. MG LC 78/2004, PR LC 176/2014), not listed.
- **Mexico:** Ciudad de México and about 20 other states.
- **Argentina:** CABA, Buenos Aires, Córdoba, Santa Fe (1984 rules cited, not online), Mendoza and others.
- **India:** Tamil Nadu, Karnataka, West Bengal, Punjab, Gujarat, Odisha, Telangana, Andhra Pradesh, Assam, Himachal Pradesh, Rajasthan, Madhya Pradesh, Uttar Pradesh, Bihar.
- **Pakistan:** Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan.
- **China:** Guangdong, Shanghai, Beijing, Zhejiang; a Chongqing 行政立法技术规范 was found in search results but cq.gov.cn could not be reached, so it is not included.
- **Other:** Nigerian states, Zanzibar, Bougainville, Tobago, Malaysian states other than Sarawak, BARMM, Nepal provinces, Sri Lanka provincial councils (a 2018 national manual for provincial statutes was launched but not verified), Colombian departments, Venezuelan states, Peruvian regions.

## Not included

Faroe Islands, Greenland and Gibraltar have no ISO 3166-2 code under their parent state. French overseas collectivities were not researched.
