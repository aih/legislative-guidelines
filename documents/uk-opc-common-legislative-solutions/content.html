Common legislative solutions: a guide to tackling recurring policy issues in legislation

This guidance is designed to help officials deal with some commonly occurring policy issues that are dealt with by adopting a commonly occurring legislative solution.

Details

This guidance has been produced with a foreword by the First Parliamentary Counsel, Jessica De Mounteney. The guidance, produced in collaboration with other legislative drafting offices in the United Kingdom, identifies commonly occurring policy issues and analyses possible legislative solutions.
