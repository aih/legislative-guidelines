Drafting instructions for the Parliamentary Counsel

Overview: Cabinet submissions seeking approval to draft a Bill require drafting instructions to be provided by the relevant agency and approved by the Parliamentary Counsel. Both of these steps (provision and approval of the instructions) are required before the submission can be uploaded as a final submission in eCabinet.

Agencies are required to complete these drafting instructions using this template. This ensures the Parliamentary Counsel has access to drafting instructions that provide sufficient guidance to commence bill drafting after Cabinet approval.

Contact details: For questions relating to this form and drafting instructions, please contact the Parliamentary Counsel’s Office at parliamentary.counsel@pco.nsw.gov.au or the Parliamentary Counsel, Annette O’Callaghan, on 0407 266 635 or 9321 3333.
