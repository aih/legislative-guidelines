Guide fédéral de jurilinguistique législative française (JLF)

The Guide fédéral de jurilinguistique législative française is a compendium of articles focused exclusively on questions pertaining to the drafting of legislative texts in French. Given the nature of this compendium, it is offered only in French. For a compendium of articles focused exclusively on questions pertaining to the drafting of legislative texts in English, please consult Legistics
