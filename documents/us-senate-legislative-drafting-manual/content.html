  • (1) IN GENERAL.-A provision is amended-

  • (B) by striking and inserting specific text.
  • (2) RESTATEMENT.- (A) IN GENERAL.-In the restatement method, an Act or subdivision of an Act is changed without specific identification of what the changes are.
  • (B) FEATURES.-This method-
  • (i) aids understanding of the effect of the provision as amended;

  • (3) STRIKE AND INSERT OF SPECIFIC TEXT.-
